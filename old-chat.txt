# Traditional 401(k) — Retirement ke baad important cases

### Case 1 — Age 60, normal withdrawal

**401(k) = $500,000**
→ $50,000 nikala
→ **10% early penalty nahi**
→ $50,000 generally **ordinary income** mein add hoga
→ income-tax bracket ke according tax pay.

---

### Case 2 — Age 55 par job chhor di

**Age = 55**
→ employer ki 401(k) se withdrawal
→ **Rule of 55 exception** apply kar sakti hai
→ qualifying plan distribution par **10% additional tax avoid ho sakta hai**
→ ordinary income tax phir bhi generally lagega.

---

### Case 3 — Age 45, emergency mein $20,000 nikala

→ $20,000 distribution
→ generally ordinary income tax
→ plus **10% additional tax**
→ $2,000 additional tax potentially.

---

### Case 4 — Age 45, rollover kar diya

Old employer 401(k) = **$100,000**
→ direct rollover to Traditional IRA / another eligible retirement plan
→ **current tax generally $0**
→ **10% early-distribution tax generally $0**
→ paisa retirement account mein hi rehta hai.

---

### Case 5 — 401(k) ka cheque khud receive kiya

401(k) = **$100,000**
→ check aapko personally mila
→ eligible rollover distribution par generally **20% federal withholding** ho sakti hai
→ full rollover karne ke liye withheld amount ko replace karna pad sakta hai
→ sahi rollover timing miss hui to taxable distribution ban sakti hai.

---

### Case 6 — 59½ se pehle medical reason

Age = **50**
→ qualifying unreimbursed medical expenses ke liye distribution
→ applicable exception ho to **10% additional tax avoid ho sakta hai**
→ taxable portion par normal income tax still possible.

---

### Case 7 — Permanent disability

Age = **48**
→ qualifying disability
→ retirement-plan distribution
→ **10% additional tax exception** potentially available
→ taxable amount par income tax generally still applies.

---

### Case 8 — 72/73/75 ke baad paisa bilkul nahi nikala

→ required minimum distributions (RMDs) start hone ke baad required amount nahi nikala
→ **excise tax risk**
→ missed RMD ko correct karna + applicable IRS rules follow karna hota hai.

---

### Case 9 — Retirement ke baad bhi kaam kar raha ho

Age = **74**
→ employer ke 401(k) mein abhi bhi working
→ plan rules aur ownership status matter karte hain
→ **still-working exception** certain situations mein current employer plan ke RMD ko delay kar sakti hai
→ previous employer ke old 401(k) par same treatment automatically assume nahi karna.

---

### Case 10 — Employer ke stock ke saath retire hua

401(k) mein company stock ki value: **$100,000**
Cost basis: **$30,000**
→ qualifying **Net Unrealized Appreciation (NUA)** strategy possible
→ stock ko special treatment mil sakta hai
→ pura amount normal ordinary-income treatment mein nahi aata
→ wrong execution se major tax difference ho sakta hai.

---

### Case 11 — 401(k) loan liya, phir job chhor di

401(k) loan balance = **$30,000**
→ job leave ki
→ loan properly repay nahi hua
→ outstanding amount **deemed distribution** ban sakta hai
→ income tax
→ age 59½ se pehle ho aur exception na ho to **10% additional tax** bhi possible.

---

### Case 12 — Hardship withdrawal

Age = **40**
→ $25,000 hardship withdrawal
→ amount generally taxable
→ hardship hone ka matlab automatically **10% additional tax se exemption nahi**.

---

### Case 13 — Retirement mein tax kam rakhna

Age = **60**
Year income low hai
→ Traditional 401(k) se **controlled amount** withdraw
→ unnecessarily huge withdrawal avoid
→ lower tax bracket mein portion taxable ho sakta hai
→ baqi money account mein continue grow kar sakta hai.

---

### Case 14 — Ek hi saal mein bohat bara withdrawal

401(k) = **$700,000**
→ ek hi year mein $300,000 withdraw
→ taxable income sharply increase
→ higher marginal tax brackets mein portion ja sakta hai
→ same money ko multiple years mein spread karne se tax result potentially better ho sakta hai.

---

### Case 15 — Traditional 401(k) → Roth conversion

Traditional account = **$200,000**
→ conversion to Roth
→ converted taxable amount generally **current-year ordinary income**
→ early-distribution penalty ka issue conversion ke mechanics se alag hota hai
→ future qualified Roth withdrawals potentially tax-free.

---

### Case 16 — Low-income retirement year mein conversion

Retirement year mein taxable income unusually low
→ Traditional 401(k)/IRA se planned Roth conversion
→ conversion amount current taxable income mein add
→ relatively low bracket use karne ka opportunity
→ future tax-deferred growth ko Roth side par move kar sakta hai.

---

### Case 17 — Inherited 401(k)

Owner ki death
→ beneficiary ko account milta hai
→ distribution/RMD rules beneficiary type aur circumstances par depend karte hain
→ surviving spouse aur non-spouse beneficiary ka treatment same nahi hota
→ wrong withdrawal timing se unexpected taxable income aa sakta hai.

---

### Case 18 — Direct rollover ke baad investment continue

Old 401(k) = **$400,000**
→ direct rollover to Traditional IRA / new plan
→ immediate tax generally avoid
→ retirement account structure continue
→ withdrawal jab hoga tab applicable tax rules apply honge.

---

### Case 19 — Roth 401(k) choose kiya hota

Same salary
→ Traditional 401(k) ke bajaye Roth 401(k) contribution
→ current contribution par Traditional jaisa tax deduction nahi
→ qualifying future Roth distribution generally tax-free
→ retirement tax planning mein useful ho sakta hai.

---

### Case 20 — Saari retirement money ek saal mein nikaal di

401(k) = **$1,000,000**
→ age 65
→ entire $1M withdrawn
→ 10% early penalty nahi
→ lekin **large taxable income** create ho sakta hai
→ federal tax + potentially state tax exposure
→ Medicare-related income effects bhi relevant ho sakte hain.

---

## Sab se dangerous practical cases

**Early withdrawal**
→ income tax + possible 10%

**Loan default**
→ distribution + possible 10%

**RMD miss**
→ excise-tax exposure

**Huge one-year withdrawal**
→ unnecessary high taxable income

**Bad rollover execution**
→ accidental taxable distribution

**Wrong NUA execution**
→ potentially large lost tax benefit

**Wrong Roth conversion amount/timing**
→ unnecessary current-year tax

**Inherited account ko ignore karna**
→ beneficiary tax/RMD problems

### Tax bachane ka practical flow

**401(k) contribution**
→ **tax deferred**
→ **investment growth**
→ retirement mein **controlled withdrawals**
→ low-income years mein **Roth conversion planning**
→ eligible cases mein **penalty exceptions**
→ proper **rollover**
→ RMDs ko time par handle karo.

Agar aapka goal **“Traditional 401(k) choose karne ke baad birth-date se death tak har IRS scenario”** hai, to main isi format mein **Age 40 → 50 → 55 → 59½ → 65 → 73 → 75 → death → spouse/children inheritance** ka complete case-flow bana sakta hoon.
